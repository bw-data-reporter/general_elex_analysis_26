### Young voters and college towns boosted Dems

**Description:** High turnout among young voters in last week's midterm election was driven by college students, giving Democratic candidates a boost in Michigan, according to a Detroit News analysis of turnout and election results data.

In Ann Arbor, 72% of registered voters cast a ballot for a turnout rate 15 percentage points higher than the presidential election two years ago and 23 percentage points higher than the 2022 midterm election.

Other cities with large public universities also saw an uptick in turnout, including East Lansing (5% higher than 2022), Allendale Township, (19% higher), Kalamazoo (6% higher), Marquette (6% higher) and Ypsilanti (24% higher).

**Data:**
 - County-level vote swings with demographics (U.S.Census Bureau, Michigan Secretary of State)
 - Turnout in youngest places in Michigan (U.S. Census Bureau, county election sites)
